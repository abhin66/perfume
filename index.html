Our Journey

Make Your Own Perfume (MYOP) is India’s first perfume bar that sells high-quality, value for money fragrances with unparalleled expertise in the art & science of perfumery.
Having roots in Calicut, a coastal town in Kerala boasting a legacy of international trade and travel since the 1800s, our journey began amidst a crowd of perfume enthusiasts who adored traditional ouds and attars.

Our early days were dedicated to thorough research and market testing to develop a blend suitable for the Indian climate. Through persistence, we curated a formula ensuring a delightful and enduring fragrance. MYOP’s first product line achieved the impossible: high-quality, value for money perfumes.

2025

Our History

2025 is a huge year for MYOP as we hit an exciting milestone – the opening of our very first international store in Mauritius!

After building a strong presence in India, we’re thrilled to take our unique perfume experience beyond borders. With 50 stores worldwide, including this brand-new one in Mauritius, we’re expanding our vision to bring personalized, high-quality perfumes to even more fragrance lovers
